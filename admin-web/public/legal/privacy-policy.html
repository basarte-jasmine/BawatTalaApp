Bawat Tala Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 23, 2026

Last Updated: April 23, 2026

Bawat Tala ("we," "our," "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, where it may go, how it is used, when counselors or admins may access it, and how we protect it when you use our mobile app, admin web panel, and related services.

This policy is intended to support compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules and regulations.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account details such as full name, email address, student number, program, gender, and address.
  • Authentication and security data such as OTP activity, password hashes, and verification status.
  • Journal data, including messages you write, generated summaries, summary notes, concern tags, and risk labels.
  • School ID images and OCR-extracted text used for verification and registration autofill.
  • Appointment, counselor, and support-follow-up records.
  • Usage metadata such as timestamps, logs, and technical information needed for support and security.
  • Google Calendar data only when an authorized admin explicitly grants calendar permission.

2. Where Your Data Goes

  • Account, journal, and appointment records are stored in Bawat Tala database and authentication systems, including PostgreSQL and Supabase-backed services used by the platform.
  • Your email address may be sent to Supabase Auth or its configured email provider to deliver OTP or password-reset messages.
  • If you upload a school ID for registration, the selected image is sent to OCR.Space so text can be extracted and checked for verification.
  • Journal text may be sent to Bawat Tala's configured AI processors, currently Google Gemini and/or Groq, to generate Muni replies, summaries, summary notes, concern labels, and safety flags.
  • Appointment-related emails may be sent through Resend or another configured mail delivery provider.
  • Google Calendar data is accessed only when an authorized admin connects Google for calendar-based appointment scheduling workflows.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage student and admin accounts.
  • To verify identity and student eligibility through school ID and email checks.
  • To secure sign-in, password reset, fraud prevention, and audit logging.
  • To operate journaling, Muni summaries, counseling support, and appointment scheduling features.
  • To detect possible safety concerns, including high-risk language or trigger words in journal entries.
  • To contact users about verification, security, appointments, and support follow-up when needed.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve service quality.

4. Journal Privacy and Counselor Access

Full journal conversations are treated as private by default and are not routinely shown to admins or counselors.

However, Bawat Tala may still generate summaries, summary notes, concern tags, and risk labels from journal entries for counseling and support workflows. These generated outputs may be visible to authorized admins or counselors even when the full conversation stays hidden.

If a journal entry is flagged by high-risk indicators, trigger words, or other safety-review signals, authorized counselors or admins may be allowed to view the full conversation so they can assess the situation, consider counseling outreach, or respond to a possible welfare concern.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with authorized school support personnel and trusted service providers that help operate the platform, and only to the extent reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy.

6. Retention and Safety Holds

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for active service use, security, support, legal compliance, and legitimate counseling operations.

If a journal entry is identified as high-risk or is under active safety review, Bawat Tala may retain that entry for counselor or admin review even if it is removed from a student's normal view inside the app.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. Passwords are stored in hashed form. While we work to protect information, no method of transmission or storage is absolutely secure.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or update of your personal information by contacting us. Some requests may be limited where retention is required for security, legal compliance, or active safety review.

9. Third-Party Services

Third-party services used by Bawat Tala, including OCR providers, AI providers, email providers, Supabase services, and Google APIs, are governed by their own terms and privacy practices. Please review them before using features that depend on those services.

10. Children's Privacy

Bawat Tala is intended for users authorized under applicable institutional and legal requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children in violation of applicable law.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates are effective when posted on this page.
